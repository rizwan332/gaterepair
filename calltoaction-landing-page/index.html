Gate Motor Repair

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you repair a gate?
We are open 24 hours a day, seven days a week, and emergency calls are dispatched as soon as a technician is free. Because the trucks carry parts for the major operator brands, most repairs are completed on the first visit rather than waiting on an order.
What types of gates do you repair?
Swing and sliding gates, driveway gates, iron and ornamental gates, and commercial and industrial entrances — residential, commercial, HOA and industrial alike.
Do you repair automatic gate openers?
Yes. Gate operators are the majority of what we do. We service LiftMaster, DoorKing, FAAC, Elite, Viking, Ramset, All-O-Matic, US Automatic and Eagle, and carry parts for them on the truck.
Do you service both residential and commercial gates?
Yes — single driveway gates through to HOA entrances, business yards and industrial sliders.
What areas do you serve?
The Dallas–Fort Worth Metroplex and surrounding areas, including Dallas, Plano, Frisco, McKinney, Irving, Garland, Arlington, Richardson, Carrollton, Mesquite, Denton, Rockwall, Allen and Grand Prairie. Call us with your address and we will confirm coverage immediately.
Do I need a new gate, or can it be repaired?
Usually it can be repaired. Most gates quoted for replacement have a failed operator, a worn hinge or a post that has shifted — all repairable. Our technician diagnoses the actual cause first and will tell you plainly if replacement genuinely is the better answer.
