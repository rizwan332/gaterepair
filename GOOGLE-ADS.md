# Google Ads Kit — Shield Gate Repair

Built so the same pages serve organic and paid. Every ad group points at a landing page whose H1 matches the ad headline — that's the Quality Score lever every competitor in this market is leaving on the table.

---

## 1. Do Local Services Ads first

LSAs (the Google Guaranteed pack) render **above** every text ad and charge **per lead, $10–$70**, not per click. In an emergency trade they intercept the highest-intent traffic before a single PPC ad renders.

**Requirements — same documents already needed for the site's trust bar:**
- Texas DPS Private Security Bureau license
- General liability insurance certificate
- Background checks on the business and every technician
- Google Business Profile with real reviews

Everlast and Dallas Automatic Gate already display license numbers, so they can qualify. **Get Shield verified before spending a dollar on PPC.** Ranking inside LSA is driven by review count and rating, proximity, responsiveness and complaint history — so answering the phone fast literally lowers cost per lead.

## 2. Campaign structure

Six campaigns. Separate budgets, separate bidding, separate intent.

| # | Campaign | Type | Intent | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Emergency / Same-Day** | Search | Broken right now, highest value | 🔴 highest bid |
| 2 | **Core Repair** | Search | Non-urgent repair | 🔴 high |
| 3 | **Brand Operators** | Search | Knows their brand — highest close rate, lowest competition | 🟢 **best ROI** |
| 4 | **Cost / Price** | Search | Researching, needs the pricing page | 🟡 medium |
| 5 | **Cities** | Search | Geo-modified | 🟡 medium |
| 6 | **Installation** | Search | High ticket, long cycle | 🟡 medium |
| — | Call-only (mobile, business hours) | Call | Emergency overflow | 🔴 |
| — | Remarketing | Display/PMax | Recover non-converters | 🟢 low spend |

**Settings across all:** Search Network only (no Display partners), geo-target DFW by radius around real service area, **Presence: people in your targeted locations** (not "interest in"), ad schedule weighted to the client's actual answer hours, call extensions on everything, mobile bid adjustment +25–40%.

## 5. Negative keyword list — build this before launch

Bleed here is the single biggest waste in home-services PPC.

```
Wrong intent:  free, diy, how to, tutorial, youtube, jobs, hiring, career, salary,
               training, course, wholesale, manufacturer, supplier, for sale, used,
               parts only, amazon, home depot, lowes, ebay, craigslist

Wrong product: garage door, garage, fence panel, fence stain, deck, patio, pergola,
               carport, dog gate, baby gate, pet gate, farm gate, cattle, security door,
               screen door, storm door, pool gate latch, gate valve, gate latch

Wrong geo:     houston, austin, san antonio, el paso, oklahoma, california,
               los angeles, phoenix   ← plus every non-DFW city that shows in the SRQ report

Careful ones:  "gate valve" (plumbing — high volume, zero relevance)
               "gate" alone (never bid broad)
               "boarding gate" / "airport gate"
               "Gate app" / "gate crypto"
```

**Review the search terms report twice a week for the first month.** In this vertical, `gate valve` and `garage door` alone can eat 30% of a new budget.

## 6. Landing page alignment — where Shield wins

Quality Score has three inputs: expected CTR, **ad relevance**, and **landing page experience**. Two of the three are page-side, and every competitor is pointing ads at slow, templated WordPress pages.

| Requirement | Shield | Competitors |
|---|---|---|
| H1 matches ad headline | ✅ per-page | ❌ generic homepage |
| Load time | ✅ static Next.js, LCP <2s | ❌ WordPress + Elementor |
| Mobile experience | ✅ mobile-first, sticky call bar | ⚠️ mixed |
| Real photos | ✅ 122 originals | ❌ mostly stock |
| Video | ✅ 25 | ❌ **zero** |
| Published pricing | ✅ | ❌ one teaser price in the market |
| Click-to-call above fold | ✅ | ⚠️ mixed |
| FAQ schema | ✅ | ❌ none |

**Expect a materially lower CPC than competitors bidding identical keywords.** That's the compounding advantage of building the site properly first.

**Rules:**
1. Never send paid traffic to the homepage. Ad group → matching page, always.
2. Every landing page has a phone number above the fold and a sticky mobile call bar.
3. Add `?utm_source=google&utm_campaign=...&utm_term={keyword}` and persist it into the `Lead` model — you need keyword-level ROI, not just campaign-level.

## 7. Conversion tracking — set up before launch, not after

1. **Call conversions from ads** — Google forwarding numbers, count calls ≥60s
2. **Call conversions from the website** — dynamic number insertion on `tel:` links
3. **Form submissions** — server-side event from `/api/leads`, so ad blockers don't hide them
4. **Enhanced conversions** — hashed phone/email from the lead form, improves attribution materially
5. **Offline conversion import** — push closed jobs and revenue back from the client's CRM. This is what lets Smart Bidding optimise for *revenue* instead of raw lead count, and it's the step nearly every local advertiser skips.
6. **GA4** — `call_click`, `form_start`, `form_submit`, `video_play`, `pricing_view`, `brand_page_view`

Add a `source` field to the `Lead` model capturing landing page, UTMs and gclid.

## 8. Budget & bidding

**Phase 1 — weeks 1–4.** Manual CPC or Maximize Clicks. Campaigns 1 (Emergency) and 3 (Brands) only. Small budget, tight exact/phrase match, aggressive negatives. Goal is clean data, not volume.

**Phase 2 — weeks 5–8.** Add Core Repair and Cost. Switch to Maximize Conversions once each campaign has ~30 conversions. Add Tier 1 city groups.

**Phase 3 — week 9+.** Target CPA or Target ROAS with offline conversion data flowing. Add Installation, remarketing, expanded cities.

Local home-services budgets typically run **$1,500–$10,000+/month** in Texas, and DFW home services is among the most competitive PPC markets in the country. Start at the low end — the brand campaign's low competition means early money goes further there than anywhere else.

**Bid priority:** Emergency > Brand > Core Repair > Cities > Cost > Installation.

## 9. Competitive notes

- **Metro Gate Repair** runs the only real offer in the market: `$200 OFF NEW GATE OPERATOR`. Counter with published pricing and a waived-diagnostic-with-repair offer — better fit for emergency intent than a discount on a purchase the customer wasn't planning.
- **automaticgaterepairdallas.com** advertises `Gate Repair – Starts at $45`. That's a service-call hook, and it will undercut Shield on price-shopper clicks. Don't chase it — beat it with transparency (full ranges) rather than a lower hook.
- Most competitors run generic "free estimate" copy. Specificity in ad copy is cheap differentiation here.
- **Do not bid competitor brand names.** Low volume in this vertical, poor conversion, and it invites retaliation from better-funded competitors like Garage Tec.
